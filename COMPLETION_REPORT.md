## 🔥 HOT TIPS FOR SUCCESS

### Content Tips
- Be **honest** about skills (don't fake!)
- Show **real projects** (not fictional)
- Write **clearly** (recruiters scan fast)
- Be **specific** (numbers > vague claims)
- Stay **professional** (check grammar)

### Portfolio Tips
- Keep it **simple** (clean beats fancy)
- Use **white space** (don't overcrowd)
- Make it **scannable** (big headings, short text)
- Be **consistent** (same style throughout)
- Test **mobile first** (many use phones)

### Deployment Tips
- Deploy **early** (don't over-perfect)
- Get **feedback** (from mentors)
- Update **regularly** (add new projects)
- Share **everywhere** (LinkedIn, resume, apps)
- Respond **quickly** (to recruiter emails)
